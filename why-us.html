Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Virtual Key Solutions — from first conversation to launch day and beyond. If you don't see your question here, we're always happy to talk.

What does Virtual Key Solutions actually do?

We design and build professional websites for local and service-based businesses — and we back them with digital marketing strategy. Think of us as architects of your digital real estate: we construct an online presence that earns trust, generates calls, and converts visitors into paying customers. Every site we build is engineered for visibility, credibility, and consistent growth.

Who do you typically work with?

We work primarily with local and service-based businesses — contractors, clinics, consultants, tradespeople, hospitality operators, and professional service providers. If your business depends on phone calls, bookings, or in-person visits, we're built for you. Our custom website design services are tailored to businesses that need real-world results, not just an online presence.

Why does a professional website matter so much?

Your website is your most visible business asset — operating 24/7 even when you're not. A professional site builds instant trust, clearly communicates your value, and guides visitors toward taking action. Without it, you're losing customers to competitors who made the investment. A poorly built site is worse than none: it signals neglect and drives people away before they ever call.

What makes Virtual Key Solutions different from other agencies?

We don't chase vanity metrics. Our focus is on outcomes that matter to your business: calls, leads, and bookings. We treat every project as a structural build — not a template swap. Our brand philosophy of 'digital real estate' means we approach your website the way a skilled developer approaches a property: with precision, purpose, and long-term value in mind. You get a dedicated team, not a ticket queue.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely — mobile optimization is non-negotiable in every project we deliver. Over 60% of web traffic comes from mobile devices, and Google ranks mobile-first. Every site we build performs seamlessly across phones, tablets, and desktops. We test rigorously across device sizes so your customers have a flawless experience regardless of how they find you.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes — redesigns are a significant part of what we do. We begin with a structural audit of your current site to diagnose what's failing and why. From there, we rebuild with purpose: improved layout, stronger messaging, faster load times, and a clear conversion path. We preserve what's working and replace what isn't. Most clients see immediate improvements in engagement and lead quality after a redesign.

Do you help with Google visibility and local SEO?

Yes. Every site we build is structured for search engine visibility from the ground up — proper heading hierarchy, fast load speeds, clean code, and on-page SEO fundamentals. We also offer dedicated local SEO services including Google Business Profile optimisation, local citation building, and keyword targeting for your service area. Being found in your digital location is just as important as having a great site.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes, completely. Your initial consultation with us carries no cost and no obligation. We use that time to understand your business, your goals, and where your current digital presence is falling short. You'll leave with clarity on what's needed — whether you choose to work with us or not. We believe in earning your trust before asking for your investment.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most projects are completed within 2–4 weeks from the time we have all the necessary content and approvals in hand. Complex builds with custom functionality or larger site architectures may take 4–6 weeks. We provide a clear project timeline at the outset and keep you informed at every stage. Speed matters to us — but never at the cost of quality or structural integrity.

Do I need to provide my own content and images?

We work with whatever you have — and fill the gaps where you don't. If you have brand photos and written copy ready, we'll incorporate them seamlessly. If you need content written or professional imagery sourced, we offer copywriting and curated stock photography as part of our service packages. Our goal is to remove every obstacle between you and a finished, high-performing website.

What is a 'preview period' and how does it work?

Before your site goes live, we share a private staging link so you can review every page in detail. This preview period gives you the opportunity to request adjustments, refine copy, and confirm every element meets your expectations. We don't launch until you're satisfied. It's our quality checkpoint — ensuring the final product is exactly what was agreed upon, with no surprises.

Who owns the website once it's built?

You do — fully and completely. Upon final payment, all design assets, content, and website files belong to you. We don't lock you into proprietary systems that hold your site hostage. Your domain, your hosting account, your business. We believe in building relationships based on results, not dependency. You stay because we deliver value — not because you have no choice.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing maintenance and support packages for clients who want peace of mind after launch. This covers software updates, security monitoring, minor content edits, and performance checks. Think of it as a property management service for your digital real estate — keeping everything running smoothly so you can focus on your business. One-off support is also available as needed.

How is pricing structured?

Our pricing is project-based and transparent — no hidden fees, no surprise invoices. Every proposal includes a detailed scope of work so you know exactly what you're getting and what it costs. We offer packages suited to different business sizes and needs, from foundational digital storefronts to full custom website design and development builds with integrated marketing. Contact us for a tailored quote specific to your goals.

What platforms do you build on?

We build on platforms best suited to your business needs — most commonly WordPress, with custom themes and no bloated page builders that slow sites down. For e-commerce requirements, we work with Shopify and WooCommerce. Our recommendations are always based on what will perform best for your specific use case, not what's easiest for us to build on. The right foundation matters.

How do I get started?

Getting started is straightforward. Reach out via our contact page or call us directly to book your free consultation. We'll discuss your business, your goals, and your current digital situation. From there, we put together a tailored proposal. No pressure, no hard sell — just a clear picture of what's possible and what it takes to get there. Your digital real estate is waiting to be built.
